About me

I am Matthew Reyna and I am a senior computer engineering undergraduate who joined the department of Electrical and Computer Engineering in Spring 2024 at Texas Tech University, where I've developed a strong foundation in embedded systems and software development. Currently, I'm working on my senior capstone project focused on developing a photodiode transimpedance amplifier circuit to detect different light levels from a CRT monitor..

During my academic career, I've maintained a 3.5 GPA while gaining hands-on experience in digital systems, signal processing applications, and analog circuit design. I've served as an engineering mentor for the LYFE program at Texas Tech University as well as an acedemic tutor for ECE3311 (Electronics I).

My technical interests center around control systems and embedded systems. After my Fall 2026 graduation I will be working full-time at Lockheed Martin, as a Systems Integration and Test Engineer in Fort Worth, Texas.

Beyond academics, I like to spend my free time with my wife, playing video games such as DOOM, going to the gym, or reading my Bible.
